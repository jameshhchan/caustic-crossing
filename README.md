# Caustic Crossing

**▶ Play: [jameshhchan.github.io/caustic-crossing](https://jameshhchan.github.io/caustic-crossing/)**

A game about quasar microlensing. Stars in a distant galaxy sweep their caustics across a quasar. Map its black hole's disk from the flicker.

Each event is a real system: a black hole of a given mass, a lens galaxy and a quasar at their own redshifts, and an observing window from a 100-day close-up of one caustic crossing to a 10-year survey. Watch the light curve, tune your disk model until it matches, then lock in and see the true disk.

## What you fit

- **Size:** the half-light radius r½ at rest-frame 2500 Å
- **Shape:** inclination i and orientation θ
- **Inner edge:** r_in, and the lower limit it puts on the black hole's spin
- **GR effects:** Doppler boosting and gravitational redshift, on or off
- **Temperature slope:** T ∝ r^−β, with up to six Rubin LSST bands (ugrizy)

Choose Easy, Medium, Hard or Extreme, or set your own rules in Custom. Earn points and climb from Newbie to Einstein's favourite. Daily and Timed modes are there for a challenge.

## Requirements

A recent desktop browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari). Everything runs in the browser, with nothing to install.
